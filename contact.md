# Contact

Questions about the mzPeak format, its specification, or the tooling around it? This is where everyone gets support on the format.

## Get in touch

### [info@mzpeak.org](mailto:info@mzpeak.org)

Email us to get help adopting or implementing mzPeak, to ask about the specification, to report a problem, or to discuss the format with the community.

mzPeak is developed as an open community effort under **[HUPO-PSI](https://www.psidev.info/)** — anyone working with the format is welcome to reach out. For code-level issues you can also use the relevant [GitHub repositories](https://github.com/HUPO-PSI/mzPeak), and for the specification itself the [specification repository](https://github.com/HUPO-PSI/mzPeak-specification).

> Want to **support** mzPeak as an institution, vendor, or software project? See [Supporters](/supporters) — or just email [info@mzpeak.org](mailto:info@mzpeak.org).
